# Business Problem & Objectives

## Business Problem

A retail company sources products across five categories (Fashion, Home, Electronics, Food, Health) from thousands of suppliers, storing and distributing inventory through a network of regional warehouses. Leadership lacks a single, unified view of:

- Which suppliers are reliable vs. underperforming
- Which warehouses are over- or under-utilized
- Where inventory is at risk of stockout or overstock
- Where shipping delays and costs are concentrated
- Which products need reordering, and how urgently

Data currently exists only as raw records with no consolidated reporting layer, no standard KPIs, and no mechanism for non-technical stakeholders to ask questions of the data directly.

## Objectives

1. **Consolidate** raw supply chain data into a clean, query-ready SQL database.
2. **Quantify** supply chain health using standard KPIs: inventory turnover, fill rate, on-time delivery rate, warehouse utilization, supplier performance.
3. **Visualize** these KPIs in an executive-ready, multi-page Power BI dashboard with drill-through from company-level to warehouse/supplier/product-level detail.
4. **Explain**, not just report — use an AI assistant layered on top of the calculated metrics to answer natural-language questions ("why are deliveries delayed?", "which warehouse is underperforming?") and generate automated business recommendations.
5. **Demonstrate** end-to-end analytics capability spanning SQL, Python, BI tooling, and applied AI — the skill set expected of Data Analyst, BI Analyst, Business Analyst, and AI Analyst roles.

## Target Audience (roles this project demonstrates)

| Role | What this project shows |
|---|---|
| Data Analyst | Data cleaning, SQL querying, KPI derivation |
| BI Analyst | Power BI dashboard design, DAX, UX for executives |
| Business Analyst | Translating metrics into business recommendations |
| AI Analyst | Applying LLMs to structured business data for Q&A and insight generation |
